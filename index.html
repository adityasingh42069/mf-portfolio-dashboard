Question 3

What do investors pay, who charges the most, and which funds give the most return per rupee of cost?

Returns shown everywhere in this dashboard are already net of the expense ratio, because the ratio is deducted from the NAV every day.

What an investor pays

Only the expense ratio goes to the AMC and its service providers. The rest are statutory or go back into the scheme.

Expense ratio by asset category

Average TER, direct plan against regular plan of the same scheme. The gap is mostly distributor commission.

Which AMC charges the most?

Best return per unit of expense

Annualised return divided by the direct-plan TER. Funds under ₹100 crore are left out. Passive funds win on this ratio almost by construction, so the active list is shown separately.
